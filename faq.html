Everything you need to plan your stay.

What rooms are available?

Economy, Standard and Executive room categories are available.

How do I make a booking?

Submit a stay request through the booking form or contact Teteman View directly through WhatsApp or phone.

Is my booking automatically confirmed?

No. A website booking is a request. The hotel must confirm availability before a reservation is confirmed.

Can I call the hotel directly?

Yes. Call +233 24 546 7920.

Where is Teteman View Hotel located?

MWPG+9G3, Accra, Ghana.

Does the hotel have Wi-Fi?

Yes. Free Wi-Fi is among the confirmed room facilities.

What facilities are available in the rooms?

Flat-screen television, work desk, telephone, free Wi-Fi, hot and cold water, private bathroom, air conditioning, refrigerator, wardrobe and room service.

Does the hotel have a restaurant?

Yes. Teteman View Hotel has a restaurant.
